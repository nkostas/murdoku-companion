How to play

  • At most one suspect may be placed in each row and column. Some rows or columns may remain empty.
  • Left click: add or remove a candidate position.
  • Left drag: add or remove multiple candidate positions. The first cell determines the action.
  • Right click / drag: add or remove a global X, independent of the selected suspect.
  • Long left: place the selected suspect or note symbol. The rest of its row and column are excluded automatically.
  • Candidate positions for all suspects remain visible.
  • Suspect cards: Left click selects; right click applies the last-used ribbon or cycles the current ribbon; Ctrl+click cycles ribbon colors; Shift+click cycles gender; long press changes the letter.
  • Blocked cells are optional and are only needed when they are known.
